Removent brings your remote desktop closer, with a native Mac workspace and no hosted account requirement.

[Get Removent](/download) · [Read the documentation](/docs)

Connect over your LAN, configure your own relay for access across networks, or connect to an existing VNC or RDP server. Available for Apple silicon Macs running macOS 13 or later.
